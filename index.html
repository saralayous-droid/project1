Recent News

Astronomical Phenomena

2001 15 4

A total eclipse can occur, where the moon completely covers the sun, or a partial eclipse, where the moon covers only part of the sun. This phenomenon is one of the amazing natural events that arouse the interest of scientists and astronomical observers around the world .

Space Missions

1990 10 3

The Deep Well Probe (James Webb Space) is one of the most prominent modern space missions. This probe was launched in December 2021 and aims to study the universe in infrared spectra, which allows it to see distant stars and galaxies that are difficult to observe by conventional telescopes.

New discoveries

2018 8 5

In an exciting recent discovery, astronomers announced the observation of a strange planet called “Kepler-452b”, which is nicknamed “Twin Earth” due to the great similarity between it and our planet. This planet is located in a habitable area around a star .
